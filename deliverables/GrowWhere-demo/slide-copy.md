## Slide 6 — What makes GrowWhere different?

- **Compare two fields.** Keep one field fixed while exploring another with the same crop and water scenario.
- **Trace the calculation.** Export the seven-factor index, full FAO-56 equations and data limits in a PDF.
- **Load prepared maps.** Read versioned scores from SQLite; show No data where inputs are missing.

Visual: `screenshots/field-comparison.jpg`.

## Slide 7 — Future plans

- **Validated coverage.** Add compatible soil and land-cover inputs before publishing scores for more regions.
- **Finer prepared layers.** Precompute detailed layers and automate versioned data refreshes.
- **Field measurements and planning.** Add soil tests, local sensors and water costs to support validated recommendations.
- **Winter crops and multiple years.** Extend the method to overwintering crops and multi-year climate assessment.
